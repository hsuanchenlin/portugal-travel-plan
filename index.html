Essential Travel Tips

Make the most of your Portugal adventure

Book in Advance

Pena Palace and Livraria Lello require advance tickets, especially in March. Book at least 2-3 weeks ahead to secure your preferred time slots.

Wear Comfortable Shoes

Both Porto and Lisbon are hilly cities with cobblestone streets. Comfortable walking shoes are essential for exploring.

Must-Try Foods

Don't miss: Francesinha in Porto, Pastel de Nata in Belém, fresh seafood, and Portuguese wine. Try petiscos (tapas) at local tascas!

Getting Around

Get a rechargeable Andante card in Porto and Viva Viagem card in Lisbon for public transport. Both cities are very walkable!

Tipping Culture

Tipping is not mandatory in Portugal. Round up at cafes, 5-10% at restaurants if service was good. Tour guides appreciate €5-10.

March Weather

Expect 10-18°C with possible rain. Layered clothing recommended. Spring blooms make this a beautiful time to visit!

Boa Viagem! 🇵🇹

Have an amazing time exploring Portugal. Feel free to modify this itinerary based on your preferences and energy levels.
